PEER STARSHIP

Crew the Wayfarer together. Find three relics on the sector's worlds and bring them home to the starbase, while raiders hunt you. Phones run the bridge stations, a big screen is the viewscreen, and crew walk the decks. No server: everyone's browser is the ship.

Checking for a VR headset…

How to run a station The tabs along the top switch stations, and show who's at each. Everyone sees the hull, the shields and red alert.
  • Helm flies the ship: drag the impulse bar, drag round the compass or hold PORT and STBD. Tap the map for a waypoint, then AUTOPILOT or WARP to it. ORBIT a planet so the transporter can reach it; DOCK at the starbase to repair and rearm (and to cast off at the start).
  • Tactical fights: tap a raider on the radar to target it, fire PHASERS when it's in the orange arc and they've charged, and TORPEDOES from loaded tubes. Raise SHIELDS in a fight, but the transporter won't work through them.
  • Science scans planets for relics and raiders for their shield harmonics (tactical does half again the damage), works the TRANSPORTER (beam crew on the pad down, and the away team back up with the relic), and chooses what's ON SCREEN.
  • Engineering shares out eight pips of power: more to engines flies faster, to weapons charges them quicker, to shields makes them stronger, to sensors reaches further and locks on faster. Tap a damaged room to send the damage control team.
How to crew the ship Hits on the hull break things: sparking conduits and fires in the rooms of the systems they hurt, which work worse until they're mended.
  • Fix sparks with the spanner and put fires out with the extinguisher, from close up.
  • Load torpedoes: take one from the rack in the torpedo room and carry it to an empty tube. Faster than the autoloader.
  • Away missions: stand on the transporter pad while the ship orbits a relic world. On the surface, shoot the drones with your phaser, take the relic from its plinth, and have science beam you up.
  • Run a station from a console on the bridge (desktop and phone).
  • Desktop: WASD, mouse, Click use the tool, 1 2 3 phaser, spanner, extinguisher, E take, load, pick up, sit. VR: phaser on your right hip, spanner on your left, extinguisher on your chest; A takes and loads. Touch: thumbs walk and look, USE and ACT.
About the viewscreen Open this page on a big screen the bridge can see. It shows forward, aft, tactical, the target, or over the away team's shoulders: science chooses, or press 1-5 here. In a headset you ride on the hull.

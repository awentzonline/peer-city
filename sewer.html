SEWER LORDZ

Down the manhole with your mates, into the filth, for the riches that end up down there. Goblins want them too. No server: everyone's browser is the sewer.

Checking for a VR headset…

How to be a Lord Everyone gathers at the foot of the ladder, and down you go. The sewage hides the bottom, so there's no seeing what's down there:
  • The metal detector beeps faster over loot buried in the silt. Dig it up and it goes in your sack. Carry it back to the ladder and it's banked.
  • Goblins come out of the drains to scratch you and snatch loot out of your sack. Punch them and they fly. Wind up a haymaker and they burst. Grab one and throw it, or pull it in half.
  • The fatberg plugs the tunnel to the vault, where the best stuff is. Pump your pressure hose and blast your way through.
  • The water rises. Turn the relief valves to let it away, or you'll be swimming in it. Head under too long and you're down: a friend has to haul you up.
  • It's over when nobody's left on their feet. Climb out with the haul to keep it.
Desktop: WASD wade, Click punch (hold to wind up), E grab, or hold to dig, turn a valve, haul someone up or climb out. 1 2 3 fists, detector, hose. R pumps, Click sprays. Holding a goblin, Click throws it and right-click tears it.
VR: swing your fists. Grip to grab: a goblin, a valve's wheel, loot in the muck. Two hands on a goblin, pull apart. The hose is on your right hip: grip its slide with your other hand and pump. A hauls up and climbs.
Touch: left thumb wades, right looks, PUNCH, GRAB, TOOL and PUMP.

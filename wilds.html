PEER WILDS

Survive on a wild island with no server: hunt, farm, keep a fire going through the night. Tools are hands-on in VR and point-and-click on desktop, in the same world.

Checking for a VR headset…

How to play You start in a meadow with an axe, a bow, a hoe, arrows, seeds and a few carrots. Food runs down; eat to stay alive, and a full stomach heals you. Nights are cold and bring wolves: they won't come near a lit fire.
  • Axe: chop trees for logs, strike animals and survivors, butcher carcasses for meat.
  • Bow: hunt deer and rabbits. Missed arrows can often be picked up again.
  • Sneak (hold C, or crouch in VR) and creep up behind an animal to strike it with the axe or hoe before it notices: a sneak attack does triple damage.
  • Hoe tills soil and hits too, seeds go in tilled soil, and carrots are ripe in a couple of minutes.
  • Pack: what you gather (seeds, food, logs) goes into your pack instead of cluttering your hands. Press B to open it and click to move things in and out; in VR, reach behind a shoulder and squeeze. You carry it all either way.
  • Logs: three build a fire, one more keeps it burning. Cook raw meat over it.
VR: everything is on your body, so squeeze a grip by your shoulders, back or belt to take it. Swing the axe into a trunk, chop the hoe into the ground. To shoot, hold the bow in one hand, take an arrow from over your right shoulder, touch it to the bow, hold the trigger, draw back and let go. Hold food to your mouth to eat and raw meat over the flames to cook. Reach down and squeeze an empty hand on a ripe carrot to pull it. Health, food and a map are on your left wrist, and Y hangs a settings panel in front of you: touch a row and pull the trigger to turn your microphone on or mute someone.

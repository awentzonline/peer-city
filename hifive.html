How to give five
  • Hold one out: up high, down low, a fist bump, or a double. Someone (a friend or one of the team) swings at it.
  • Swing at someone's: face them and press the same move as the ring closes on the target. Perfect timing scores most.
  • Combos: five after five with the same person multiplies your points. Mix up the moves to keep it climbing.
  • Too slow! Pull your offer away just as they swing, and you get the points instead.
  • Butt slaps: get behind someone and slap. Crouch so they don't hear you coming. If they turn round in time, it's denied and they score. Turn slaps off in the settings if you'd rather not.
Desktop: 1 (or click) up high, 2 (or right-click) down low, 3 fist bump, 4 double, Q pull it away, F slap, C sneak, WASD walk, Space jump (fives in the air are worth more).
VR: it's your hands. Hold one still over your head to offer (both for a double), out low for down low, a fist (squeeze the grip) at your chest for a bump. Swing into theirs: the faster, the better.
Touch: swipe the pad: up for up high, down for down low, sideways for a double, tap for a fist bump. SLAP and SNEAK are by your thumb.

PEER CITY 3D

A serverless first-person open-world sandbox, playable on desktop or in room-scale VR. Every player's browser simulates the city together.

Checking for a VR headset…

Touch controls Your left thumb walks: push it all the way to run. Your right thumb looks, and a tap where you're looking fires, so a second finger shoots without moving your aim. FIRE holds down the trigger for automatic weapons. USE takes the car you're standing by and gets you back out; in a car the same buttons become BRAKE, HORN and CAM. Tap a gun in the strip on the right to switch to it. The cog opens settings and the microphone lets players near you hear your voice.
VR controls Walk around your room for real, or use the left stick to move. Right stick snap-turns. Squeeze a grip to grab a gun off your body: the pistol is on your right hip, the SMG on your left, long guns over your shoulders. Let go with your hand against your torso to stash it right there. Trigger fires. Walk over a gun to pick it up. A/X enter or exit a car. Driving: left stick steers and accelerates, right stick click handbrake, B horn, Y recenter your seat. On foot, Y hangs a settings panel in front of you: touch a row and pull the trigger to turn your microphone on or mute someone. Your stats and minimap are on your left wrist.

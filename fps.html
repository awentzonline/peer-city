PEER CITY 3D

A serverless first-person open-world sandbox, playable on desktop or in room-scale VR. Every player's browser simulates the city together.

Checking for a VR headset…

VR controls Walk around your room for real, or use the left stick to move (click or grip to run). Right stick snap-turns. Each trigger fires the gun in that hand, and B switches guns. Walk over a gun to pick it up. A/X enter or exit a car. Driving: left stick steers and accelerates, right grip handbrake, B horn, Y recenter your seat. Your stats and minimap are on your left wrist.

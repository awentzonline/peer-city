PEER WALLS

A yard of blank walls and a rack of spray paint. Bring your friends and cover them. No server: everyone's browser is the yard.

Checking for a VR headset…

How to paint You've got a spray can, a marker and a roller. Hold the mouse button to paint where the crosshair points.
  • Spray goes on thicker the longer you hold it on a spot, and wider and softer the further back you stand. Move fast for a light dusting, slow for solid colour.
  • The marker and roller only work right up against the wall. The roller is for big fills.
  • Q and E change size: skinny to fat caps, fine to broad nibs, narrow to wide rollers.
  • The wheel changes colour, or point at a swatch on the rack by the gate and click it. Buff is the wall's own grey, for painting over mistakes.
WASD walk, Shift runs, C crouches for the bottom of the wall, 1 2 3 pick a tool, Esc settings, V microphone.

VR: the spray can is on your right hip, the marker on your left, the roller over your left shoulder. Squeeze a grip there to take one and pull the trigger to paint. A and B change colour, X the size, Y opens the settings.

Touch: turn DRAW on and paint with your finger wherever you touch the wall; turn it off to look around. Tap colours along the bottom and tools up the right.

Friends who join see everything already painted. Your browser keeps the walls too, so they're still there when you come back to an empty yard.
